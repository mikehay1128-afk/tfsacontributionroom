Step-by-Step Guide

How to Determine Your TFSA Contribution Room

Follow this calculation formula to accurately measure your available room and avoid CRA penalty taxes.

  1. Determine your starting eligibility year

    Your contribution room starts accumulating in the year you turn 18, provided you were a Canadian resident with a valid SIN. The earliest year room could accumulate is 2009.

  2. Calculate cumulative TFSA annual limits

    Sum up all annual limits for the years you were eligible. If you were eligible from 2009 to 2026, your total maximum cumulative limit is $109,000.

  3. Subtract all lifetime TFSA deposits

    Deduct every dollar you have deposited into any TFSA account across all banks and institutions. Note that market growth inside your account does not reduce room.

  4. Add back prior-year withdrawals

    Add back any withdrawals made in previous calendar years. Withdrawals made in the current calendar year will be restored on January 1 of next year.

What Is TFSA Contribution Room?

Your TFSA contribution room is the total amount you can contribute to all your Tax-Free Savings Accounts combined. Unlike RRSP room which depends on income, TFSA room is a flat amount assigned to all eligible Canadian residents every year.

TFSA Annual Contribution Limits (2009–2026)

Below are the historical limits set by the CRA:

  • 2009–2012: $5,000 per year
  • 2013–2014: $5,500 per year
  • 2015: $10,000
  • 2016–2018: $5,500 per year
  • 2019–2022: $6,000 per year
  • 2023: $6,500
  • 2024–2026: $7,000 per year
⚠️ CRA Verification Notice: For official TFSA records, log in to CRA My Account. Note that CRA system records are updated in spring for the previous year; track recent deposits manually using this calculator.
